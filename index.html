Registration rules

  • Registration is completely free.
  • One registration per student per game.
  • Enter every team member only once.
  • Team size must match the selected game.
  • Registration closes after 15 October.
🔡

Register number format

Your register number must match this exact pattern:

NNLNNLNNNN

Example: 25B81A4605

Digits at positions 1–2, 4–5 and 7–10. Uppercase letters only at positions 3 and 6. Exactly 10 characters.
